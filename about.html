Our Ethical Standards

How Gourshal Stands Apart

In a world obsessed with industrial speed and high yields, we honor patience, natural seasons, and hand craftsmanship.

🌱

Ethical Direct Sourcing

We partner directly with traditional gaushalas, mountain tea estates, and tribal honey foragers, paying fair above-market compensation.

🪵

Zero Machine Refining

No chemical solvents, synthetic bleaching, or micro-filtering. We extract our products using slow wooden churns and low flames.

🔬

100% NABL Lab Certified

Every single batch is tested for zero adulteration, pesticides, or heavy metals. Complete transparency in every single jar.

Our Craft & Heritage

The Pillars of Vedic Purity

Rooted in ancient Indian wisdom, crafted with uncompromised food integrity.

Pillar 01 • Sacred Gaushala

A2 Gir Cow Vedic Bilona Ghee
